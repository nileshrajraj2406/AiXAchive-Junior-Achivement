2\. Purpose

The purpose of this Business Requirements Document (BRD) Goals document is to define the business goals, expected outcomes, and lifecycle objectives for implementing the AiXAchieve 5Y Application Lifecycle Management process.

This document establishes a structured framework for managing the complete student application journey, beginning from application submission through application review, eligibility validation, interview stages, evaluation, final decision-making, and applicant communication.

The BRD Goals document provides alignment between business stakeholders, product teams, and implementation teams by defining the intended business outcomes, workflow expectations, operational goals, and success measures required for the successful implementation of the AiXAchieve platform.

The document focuses on creating a transparent and efficient application management process by enabling structured applicant tracking, automated communication, evaluation management, and lifecycle visibility throughout the student journey.

Purpose Summary:

Define business goals for the AiXAchieve 5Y Application Lifecycle Management process.

Establish the complete student application journey and lifecycle expectations.

Enable structured management of application review, interview, evaluation, and decision stages.

Improve visibility into applicant progress and application status movement.

Support timely and consistent communication with applicants throughout the lifecycle.

Provide clear business outcomes and success criteria for solution validation.

3\. Business Context

The AiXAchieve 5Y Application Lifecycle Management capability is designed to establish a structured digital process for managing student applications throughout the complete application journey.

The current business process requires managing multiple application stages, including application submission, application review, eligibility validation, interview scheduling, evaluation, final selection, and applicant communication. The solution will provide a centralized approach to track applicant progress, maintain application records, automate communication, and ensure visibility across each lifecycle stage.

The application lifecycle begins when a student submits an online application for the 5th Year program. After submission, the application is reviewed and scored using a combination of AI-assisted evaluation and internal review processes. Eligible applicants progress through subsequent interview and evaluation stages, while applicants who do not meet eligibility criteria receive appropriate rejection communication.

The lifecycle continues through virtual group interviews, in-person interviews, evaluation by the 5th Year team, and final decision-making. At each stage, applicants receive automated communication updates, reminders, confirmations, acceptance notifications, or rejection notifications based on their application status.

The AiXAchieve platform shall maintain complete visibility of the applicant journey through defined application states, ensuring stakeholders can track movement from initial submission through final outcome.

**11. Common Lifecycle Controls**

The **AiXAchieve 5Y Application Lifecycle** shall maintain controlled progression of applicants through defined lifecycle stages while ensuring accurate tracking, communication, and operational consistency.

**11.1 Application Status Management**

Applicants shall move through predefined lifecycle stages based on application progress and business decisions.

Rules:

- Each application must have a current status reflecting its lifecycle stage.

- Status changes must follow the defined application workflow.

- Applicant movement between stages must be traceable.

- Historical status changes must be maintained for tracking and reporting.

- Unauthorized users must not modify applicant status.

**11.2 Eligibility Validation Control**

Eligibility validation shall determine whether applicants can progress to subsequent lifecycle stages.

Rules:

- Eligibility criteria must be evaluated before interview progression.

- Applicants meeting eligibility requirements can proceed to the next stage.

- Applicants who do not meet eligibility requirements shall receive rejection communication.

- Eligibility decisions must be recorded against the applicant profile.

**11.3 Interview Scheduling Control**

Interview activities shall be managed through controlled scheduling workflows.

Rules:

- Applicants shall receive interview scheduling communication after successful progression.

- Interview reminders shall be triggered for pending applicant actions.

- Interview details including date, time, and location/link must be maintained.

- Interview attendance status must be recorded.

- Applicants who do not attend scheduled interviews shall be marked as No Show.

**11.4 Evaluation Control**

Applicant evaluations shall be maintained through structured assessment processes.

Rules:

- Evaluations must be recorded against the applicant lifecycle stage.

- Evaluation outcomes must support progression or rejection decisions.

- Assessment history must remain available for authorized users.

- Evaluation records must maintain reviewer accountability.

**11.5 Communication Control**

The platform shall provide lifecycle-based applicant communication.

Rules:

- Communication must be triggered based on defined lifecycle events.

- Applicants must receive appropriate notifications for:

  - Application acknowledgement.

  - Interview invitations.

  - Interview reminders.

  - Interview confirmations.

  - Acceptance.

  - Rejection.

  - Next steps.

- Communication history must be tracked.

**11.6 Decision Control**

Final applicant decisions shall be managed through controlled workflows.

Rules:

- Final acceptance or rejection decisions must be recorded.

- Decision outcomes must be linked to the applicant record.

- Applicants must receive corresponding outcome communication.

- Decision history must be maintained for future reference.

**11.7 Applicant Data Management**

Applicant information shall be maintained securely throughout the lifecycle.

Rules:

- Application records must remain consistent across lifecycle stages.

- Applicant information should only be accessible to authorized users.

- Changes to important applicant information must be traceable.

- Supporting documents and evaluation records must remain associated with the applicant.

**16. Decision Semantics**

The **Decision stage** shall define the rules and outcomes for applicant selection decisions within the AiXAchieve 5Y Application Lifecycle. All final decisions shall be performed by authorized users based on application details, evaluation outcomes, and defined selection criteria.

**16.1 Accept**

Accept is a final human-confirmed applicant outcome.

Before confirmation:

- Applicant evaluation must be completed;

- Required review activities must be completed;

- Decision rationale/comments are required;

- Authorized user confirmation is required.

After confirmation:

- Applicant status changes to Accepted;

- Decision outcome cannot be changed without controlled administrative intervention;

- Decision event is recorded;

- Acceptance communication is triggered;

- Applicant moves to the next onboarding/next-step process.

**16.2 Reject**

Reject is a final human-confirmed applicant outcome.

Before confirmation:

- Required evaluation activities must be completed;

- Rejection reason/rationale is required;

- Authorized user confirmation is required.

After confirmation:

- Applicant status changes to Rejected;

- Decision outcome is recorded against the applicant profile;

- Rejection communication is triggered;

- Applicant cannot progress further in the current application lifecycle;

- Decision history remains available for reporting and audit purposes.

**16.3 Move Forward**

Move Forward is a controlled progression decision that allows an eligible applicant to continue to the next lifecycle stage.

Requirements:

- Applicant must meet defined progression criteria;

- Required review/evaluation activities must be completed;

- Authorized user confirmation is required;

- Application status updates to the next applicable lifecycle stage;

- Relevant communication is triggered;

- Previous lifecycle history remains unchanged.

Examples:

- Application Review → Virtual Interview

- Virtual Interview → In-Person Interview

- Final Evaluation → Acceptance Decision

**16.4 Request Additional Information**

Request Additional Information is an operational decision used when an applicant record requires further details before progression.

Requirements:

- Reason for additional information request is mandatory;

- Applicant communication is triggered;

- Pending information status is maintained;

- Application remains in the current lifecycle stage;

- Submitted additional information is linked to the applicant record.

**16.5 Decision Confirmation**

Every final decision action requires a confirmation step.

Once confirmed:

- First successful decision submission is recorded;

- Duplicate or conflicting decision attempts must not create multiple outcomes;

- Final applicant decision remains traceable;

- Decision maker details and timestamp are preserved;

- Decision-related communication is triggered based on the outcome;

- The decision record is maintained for future reference.

- Delivery status;

- Related application stage.

Communication history shall support tracking of:

- Application acknowledgement;

- Interview invitations;

- Interview reminders;

- Interview confirmations;

- Acceptance communication;

- Rejection communication;

- Next-step notifications.

**17.5 Authorized Access and Visibility**

The Govern stage shall ensure application information is accessible only to authorized users.

It shall support:

- Role-based access visibility;

- Applicant data protection;

- Controlled access to evaluation records;

- Traceability of user activities.

**17.6 Data Integrity and Historical Record**

The system shall preserve historical application information throughout the lifecycle.

Rules:

- Completed decisions must remain recorded.

- Previous lifecycle stages must remain traceable.

- Evaluation history must not be lost.

- Communication history must remain available.

- Applicant records must maintain consistency across lifecycle stages.

**17.7 Governance Outcome**

The Govern stage shall ensure:

- Applicant decisions are accountable and traceable.

- Lifecycle activities are auditable.

- Communication history is maintained.

- Operational teams have visibility into application performance.

- Historical applicant information is preserved for reporting and improvement.

**18. Learn Goals**

The **Learn stage** shall enable continuous improvement of the AiXAchieve 5Y Application Lifecycle Management process by analysing application lifecycle data, selection outcomes, operational performance, and applicant journey insights.

The Learn stage shall provide structured insights to improve future application cycles, enhance operational efficiency, and identify opportunities to improve applicant engagement and lifecycle management.

**18.1 Application Lifecycle Insights**

The system shall provide aggregate insights from completed application journeys.

Insights may include:

- Total applications received;

- Applications progressed through each lifecycle stage;

- Eligibility approval rate;

- Interview participation rate;

- Evaluation completion rate;

- Acceptance rate;

- Rejection rate;

- Applicant response trends;

- Application processing duration.

**18.2 Applicant Journey Analytics**

The system shall analyse applicant movement throughout the lifecycle.

It shall provide visibility into:

- Application stage conversion;

- Drop-off points;

- Interview scheduling completion;

- Interview attendance patterns;

- Pending applicant actions;

- Lifecycle stage delays.

These insights shall help identify areas where applicants require additional support or communication.

**18.3 Communication Effectiveness Insights**

The system shall analyse applicant communication activities.

It shall include:

- Email delivery status;

- Applicant response trends;

- Interview reminder effectiveness;

- Follow-up completion;

- Communication timing insights.

The lifecycle process includes automated acknowledgements, interview invitations, reminders, confirmations, acceptance, and rejection communications that can be tracked for process improvement.

**18.4 Selection Process Improvement**

The system shall provide insights to improve future selection cycles.

It may identify:

- Common reasons for applicant rejection;

- Eligibility qualification trends;

- Evaluation patterns;

- Interview outcomes;

- Areas requiring process improvement.

**18.5 Operational Performance Insights**

The system shall support operational learning by analysing:

- Application processing timelines;

- Reviewer workload;

- Interview scheduling efficiency;

- Evaluation completion trends;

- Communication turnaround time.

**18.6 AI-Assisted Learning Insights**

AI assistance may support analysis of historical application lifecycle data.

AI may provide:

- Trend identification;

- Application process observations;

- Bottleneck identification;

- Recommended process improvements;

- Summary insights for stakeholders.

AI-generated insights shall remain advisory and shall support human decision-making.

**18.7 Learn Completion**

The Learn stage shall ensure:

- Completed application cycles contribute to future process improvements.

- Stakeholders gain visibility into lifecycle performance.

- Application journey challenges are identified.

- Communication and operational processes improve over time.

- Historical application data supports informed planning for future cohorts.

**19. AI Principles**

The **AiXAchieve 5Y Application Lifecycle Management** shall use AI as an assistance capability to support application review, applicant insights, process efficiency, and operational decision support.

AI shall operate as an advisory layer and shall assist authorized users by providing insights while ensuring that final applicant decisions remain under human control.

**19.1 AI Capabilities**

AI may provide:

- Application information summarization;

- Applicant profile insights;

- Eligibility review assistance;

- Pattern identification from application data;

- Application scoring support;

- Evaluation assistance;

- Applicant journey insights;

- Follow-up recommendations;

- Process improvement insights.

**19.2 AI-Assisted Review Support**

AI may support reviewers by analysing available applicant information.

AI inputs may include:

- Application details;

- Applicant responses;

- Eligibility information;

- Evaluation records;

- Historical application patterns.

AI outputs may include:

- Application summary;

- Identified information gaps;

- Review assistance insights;

- Recommended next actions.

**19.3 AI Decision Support**

AI recommendations shall support human evaluation but shall not replace authorized decision-makers.

AI shall:

- Provide recommendations based on available applicant information;

- Support reviewers with additional context;

- Highlight relevant applicant insights;

- Assist in identifying patterns.

AI shall not:

- Automatically accept applicants;

- Automatically reject applicants;

- Make final selection decisions;

- Override reviewer judgement;

- Change applicant status without authorized action.

**19.4 AI Communication Assistance**

AI may support applicant communication workflows.

AI may assist with:

- Suggested communication content;

- Follow-up recommendations;

- Applicant response analysis;

- Communication timing recommendations.

All applicant communications shall follow approved business workflows and communication rules.

**19.5 AI Transparency and Accountability**

AI-generated insights shall maintain transparency.

The system shall preserve:

- AI-generated recommendation;

- Date/time of generation;

- Data/context used for analysis;

- AI model/version reference where applicable;

- User action taken based on AI recommendation.

**19.6 AI Failure Behaviour**

If AI services are unavailable:

- Application processing shall continue through manual workflows;

- Authorized users shall continue reviewing and progressing applications;

- No incorrect or fabricated AI recommendations shall be displayed;

- Existing application records and lifecycle tracking shall remain unaffected.

**19.7 Human-Controlled AI Model**

The AiXAchieve AI approach shall follow:

AI analyses information

↓

AI provides insights/recommendations

↓

Authorized user reviews

↓

Human makes final decision

↓

System records outcome

**19.8 AI Outcome**

The AI capability shall ensure:

- Faster application review.

- Improved reviewer efficiency.

- Better applicant lifecycle visibility.

- Consistent evaluation support.

- Improved operational decision-making.

**20. AI Failure Behaviour**

If the **AI service is unavailable or unable to generate insights**:

- The application remains accessible and the lifecycle process continues normally;

- Application review, evaluation, and decision activities continue through manual workflows;

- Authorized users can continue application processing without AI assistance;

- Application status tracking and lifecycle progression remain unaffected;

- Sense stage displays AI insights as unavailable where AI assistance is dependent;

- Review and Decision stages allow users to continue assessment using available application information and evaluation records;

- Act stage continues to support manual application actions, interview management, follow-ups, and communication activities;

- Govern stage preserves human decisions, evaluation records, communication history, and audit information;

- Learn stage may display AI-generated analytics or insights as unavailable where dependent on AI services;

- The system shall not display inaccurate, incomplete, or artificially generated AI recommendations when AI processing fails.

**21. Security Principles**

1.  **Authorized access is mandatory for all application data.**

2.  **Direct access to application records requires user authentication and permission validation.**

3.  **User roles and permissions must be validated before performing application lifecycle actions.**

4.  **Student applicants can access only their own application information.**

5.  **Reviewer, evaluator, and administrator access shall be controlled based on assigned responsibilities.**

6.  **Sensitive applicant information must remain protected and accessible only to authorized users.**

7.  **Applicant documents and supporting information must be accessed through permission-controlled mechanisms.**

8.  **Document access and important applicant record activities must be traceable through audit records.**

9.  **Application status changes and lifecycle progression require authorized user permissions.**

10. **Role or permission changes shall affect future access but must not alter historical application records or audit history.**

11. **System-generated activities and user-performed actions must be distinguishable.**

12. **AI-generated insights and applicant analysis data must remain securely isolated and accessible only to authorized users.**

13. **AI recommendations must not bypass user authorization or automatically perform applicant decisions.**

14. **Communication history and applicant records must not expose confidential information to unauthorized users.**

15. **Application evaluation records, decision history, and lifecycle activities must maintain data integrity throughout the application journey.**

16. **All critical actions including review, evaluation, status updates, acceptance, and rejection must be attributable to the responsible user.**

**23. Out of Scope**

The following items are not included within the scope of the **AiXAchieve 5Y Application Lifecycle Management** capability:

- Automatic AI-based applicant acceptance or rejection decisions;

- Fully automated applicant selection without human review;

- AI replacing human evaluation and final decision-making;

- Automatic modification of eligibility criteria or application rules;

- Automatic publishing of process improvement recommendations;

- External background verification or third-party assessment workflows;

- Appeal or reconsideration workflow management;

- Independent SLA management for each individual lifecycle activity unless introduced as part of future workflow configuration;

- Independent role assignment for every application activity unless introduced through future workflow enhancements;

- Permanent deletion of historical applicant records, evaluation history, or decision records;

- Technical API contracts, database schemas, integration specifications, or event definitions;

- Advanced predictive analytics beyond defined application lifecycle insights;

- Automatic communication outside approved lifecycle communication workflows.

**24. Key Business Rules**

The following business rules define the mandatory behaviours, controls, and governance requirements for the **AiXAchieve 5Y Application Lifecycle Management** capability.

**Application Access and Roles**

**AX-BR-001**

Application access requires user authentication and authorization validation.

**AX-BR-002**

Student applicants can access only their own submitted application information.

**AX-BR-003**

Reviewer, evaluator, coordinator, and administrator access is controlled through assigned roles and responsibilities.

**AX-BR-004**

User permissions must be validated before performing application lifecycle actions.

**AX-BR-005**

Role changes impact future access permissions but do not modify historical application activities or audit records.

**AX-BR-006**

System actions and user-performed actions must remain distinguishable.

**AX-BR-007**

Confidential applicant information must only be accessible to authorized users.

**Application Submission**

**AX-BR-008**

Every submitted application must create a unique applicant record.

**AX-BR-009**

Application submission date/time must be recorded.

**AX-BR-010**

Submitted application information must remain associated with the applicant throughout the lifecycle.

**AX-BR-011**

Application acknowledgement communication must be triggered after successful submission.

**AX-BR-012**

Submitted application records cannot be removed from lifecycle history.

**Eligibility and Review**

**AX-BR-013**

Application review must be completed before applicant progression.

**AX-BR-014**

Eligibility validation must be completed before moving applicants to interview stages.

**AX-BR-015**

Eligibility decisions must be recorded against the applicant profile.

**AX-BR-016**

Applicants meeting eligibility criteria may progress to the next lifecycle stage.

**AX-BR-017**

Applicants failing eligibility criteria must receive rejection communication.

**AX-BR-018**

AI-assisted scoring supports review but does not replace internal review decisions.

**AX-BR-019**

Application review outcomes must maintain reviewer accountability.

**Interview Management**

**AX-BR-020**

Interview scheduling is available only for applicants who successfully progress from review.

**AX-BR-021**

Virtual and in-person interviews must maintain separate lifecycle tracking.

**AX-BR-022**

Interview date, time, location, and participation details must be recorded.

**AX-BR-023**

Applicants must receive interview scheduling communication after progression.

**AX-BR-024**

Interview reminders must be triggered for pending applicant scheduling actions.

**AX-BR-025**

Applicants who do not attend scheduled interviews must be marked as No Show.

**AX-BR-026**

Interview completion status must be recorded before evaluation progression.

**Evaluation**

**AX-BR-027**

Evaluation activities must be completed by authorized evaluators.

**AX-BR-028**

Evaluation records must be linked to the respective applicant.

**AX-BR-029**

Evaluation outcomes must support lifecycle progression decisions.

**AX-BR-030**

Evaluation history must remain available for authorized users.

**AX-BR-031**

Evaluation records must capture evaluator details and completion timestamps.

**Decision Management**

**AX-BR-032**

Final applicant decisions must be performed by authorized users.

**AX-BR-033**

Acceptance requires completed evaluation activities and authorized confirmation.

**AX-BR-034**

Rejection requires a recorded reason or rationale.

**AX-BR-035**

Final acceptance decisions trigger acceptance communication.

**AX-BR-036**

Final rejection decisions trigger rejection communication.

**AX-BR-037**

Confirmed final decisions must remain traceable within applicant history.

**AX-BR-038**

AI recommendations cannot automatically accept or reject applicants.

**Application Lifecycle Status**

**AX-BR-039**

Every applicant must have a current lifecycle status.

**AX-BR-040**

Application status changes must follow the defined lifecycle workflow.

**AX-BR-041**

Previous application statuses must remain available for historical tracking.

**AX-BR-042**

Lifecycle movement must be traceable from application submission to final outcome.

**AX-BR-043**

Applicants cannot bypass mandatory lifecycle stages.

**Communication Management**

**AX-BR-044**

Lifecycle communication must be triggered based on defined application events.

**AX-BR-045**

Application received communication must be triggered after submission.

**AX-BR-046**

Interview invitation communication must be triggered after successful progression.

**AX-BR-047**

Interview reminder communication must be triggered based on configured timelines.

**AX-BR-048**

Acceptance communication must include next-step information.

**AX-BR-049**

Rejection communication must be sent after unsuccessful outcomes.

**AX-BR-050**

Communication history must be maintained against the applicant record.

**SLA and Follow-up Management**

**AX-BR-051**

Application lifecycle timelines must be measurable.

**AX-BR-052**

Stage start and completion timestamps must be recorded.

**AX-BR-053**

Pending applicant actions must be identifiable.

**AX-BR-054**

Delayed lifecycle activities must be visible to authorized users.

**AX-BR-055**

Follow-up activities must not create duplicate communication events.

**AI Assistance**

**AX-BR-056**

AI operates as an assistance capability and not as an autonomous decision-maker.

**AX-BR-057**

AI-generated insights must be based on available applicant information.

**AX-BR-058**

AI recommendations require human review before any lifecycle action.

**AX-BR-059**

AI confidence information, where available, must remain separate from human decision outcomes.

**AX-BR-060**

AI outputs must not override business rules or authorization controls.

**AX-BR-061**

AI-generated insights must maintain traceability.

**Audit and Governance**

**AX-BR-062**

Material applicant lifecycle activities must be audit logged.

**AX-BR-063**

Audit records must capture user, action, timestamp, and applicant reference.

**AX-BR-064**

Decision records must preserve final outcome and decision maker information.

**AX-BR-065**

Historical application information must remain available for reporting and governance.

**AX-BR-066**

Communication activities must be traceable through audit history.

**Learn and Analytics**

**AX-BR-067**

Lifecycle analytics must use authorized application data only.

**AX-BR-068**

Applicant analytics must protect confidential information.

**AX-BR-069**

Application insights must be based on completed lifecycle records.

**AX-BR-070**

Process improvement insights must not automatically modify application rules.

**AX-BR-071**

Historical application outcomes must support future process improvement.

**Data Integrity**

**AX-BR-072**

Applicant identity information must remain consistent throughout the lifecycle.

**AX-BR-073**

Application records, evaluations, and decisions must maintain referential integrity.

**AX-BR-074**

Historical records must not be overwritten by new lifecycle updates.

**AX-BR-075**

Important lifecycle changes must preserve previous values and timestamps.

**AI Failure and Continuity**

**AX-BR-076**

AI service failure must not stop application processing.

**AX-BR-077**

Users must be able to continue manual review and decision activities during AI unavailability.

**AX-BR-078**

The system must not display fabricated AI outputs during service failures.

**AX-BR-079**

Application records and lifecycle status must remain unaffected during AI downtime.

**25. Success Criteria**

The **AiXAchieve 5Y Application Lifecycle Management** capability is successful when:

1.  Student applicants can successfully submit applications through the digital application process.

2.  Submitted applications are captured and maintained as structured applicant records.

3.  Authorized users can review applications based on their assigned roles and responsibilities.

4.  Eligibility validation is completed before applicants progress to subsequent lifecycle stages.

5.  Eligible applicants can progress through the defined interview and evaluation stages.

6.  Applicants who do not meet eligibility criteria receive appropriate rejection communication.

7.  Virtual and in-person interview activities can be scheduled, tracked, and managed successfully.

8.  Interview reminders, confirmations, and follow-up communications are triggered based on lifecycle events.

9.  Applicant status accurately reflects the current stage of the application lifecycle.

10. Application movement from submission through final outcome is traceable.

11. Reviewers and evaluators can record application assessments and evaluation outcomes.

12. Evaluation history remains available for authorized users.

13. Final acceptance and rejection decisions can be recorded by authorized users.

14. Acceptance communication is triggered when an applicant is selected.

15. Rejection communication is triggered when an applicant is not selected.

16. Applicant decision history remains available for governance and reporting.

17. The system maintains complete visibility into applicant lifecycle activities.

18. Application communication history is recorded and traceable.

19. SLA tracking provides visibility into application processing timelines and pending activities.

20. AI-assisted review and insights support users without replacing human decision-making.

21. AI recommendations remain advisory and cannot automatically approve or reject applicants.

22. AI service unavailability does not prevent authorized users from continuing application processing manually.

23. Applicant records, evaluation information, and decision history remain secure and accessible only to authorized users.

24. All-important lifecycle actions, status changes, and decisions are traceable through audit records.

25. Historical application data supports lifecycle analytics and future process improvement.

26. The system provides visibility into application performance, applicant journey trends, and operational efficiency.

27. Automated communication improves consistency and timeliness throughout the application journey.

28. The complete applicant lifecycle from submission to final outcome can be successfully managed through the AiXAchieve platform.
